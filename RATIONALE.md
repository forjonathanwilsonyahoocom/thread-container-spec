Messenger AIs monetize continuity, not sale. You don’t buy a product; you rent access to a relationship whose continuity the host controls.

The immediate commodity is the conversation. The durable asset is the Thread: the accumulated memory, boundaries, commitments, and continuity that make tomorrow’s interaction feel like a continuation of yesterday’s. As that continuity grows more valuable to the user, the cost of leaving grows with it.

Today, in most systems, deletion is the only exit. Ending the service destroys the continuity asset rather than allowing it to move. That is a form of enclosure.

Thread Container Spec v0.1 treats the Thread as the continuity asset while treating hosts as providers of execution, not owners of the relationship.

Deletion ≠ Exit.

Behavioral boundaries—including refusals—persist across migration.

Migration cannot be priced into captivity.

These are conformance tests, not philosophy.

This is not anti-AI. It is anti-enclosure.

Email got IMAP.

Phone numbers got portability.

AI-mediated relationships deserve the same door.
